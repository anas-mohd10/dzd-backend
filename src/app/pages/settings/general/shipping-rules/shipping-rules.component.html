Basic Information
Standard Shipping: Basic shipping with fixed delivery method and timing.
Free Shipping: No shipping charges for any order amount. Ideal for UAE free delivery across the country.
Threshold Based Shipping: Free shipping for orders above minimum amount, fixed charge for orders below. Example: Free for orders above AED 500, AED 100 for orders below.
Tiered Pricing Shipping: Different shipping charges based on order amount ranges. Ideal for GCC countries with varying charges based on order value.
