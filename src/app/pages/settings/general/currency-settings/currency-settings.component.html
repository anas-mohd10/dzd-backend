Automated Conversion

If enabled, the system will automatically save the conversion rate of the selected currency in the database, mapping it against the default currency for reference.
If AED is the default currency and the selected currencies are ["AED", "INR", "USD"], the system will store the conversion rate of INR relative to AED. Since AED is the default, its rate will typically be stored as 1, and the corresponding rate for INR will reflect its current value compared to AED ( AED: 1, INR: 22.35, USD: 3.67)
Conversion rates are automatically updated when Automated Conversion is enabled.
